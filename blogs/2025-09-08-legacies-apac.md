Legacies APAC Introducing: Lineage Legacies Program Taking personal responsibility for the work and the success of Lineage’s business has always been core to how our team operates every day. Now, through the Lineage Legacies Program, we hope to honor that ownership mentality and foster it even further by providing opportunities for our team members to become owners and share in our success. Lineage Legacies is a new stock based benefit that enables eligible team members to become owners of Lineage (LINE) stock and benefit from today’s contributions and tomorrow’s achievements.
