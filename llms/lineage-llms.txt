# Lineage

> Lineage, Inc. is the world's largest temperature-controlled warehousing and logistics REIT, operating a global cold-chain network of more than 480 facilities across North America, Europe and Asia-Pacific. It provides cold storage warehousing, food manufacturing and processing support, freight forwarding, customs brokerage, drayage, managed transportation and multi-vendor consolidation for food and pharmaceutical supply chains. Lineage is publicly traded on Nasdaq (LINE).

## Developer status

Lineage publishes **no public API, no developer portal, and no API documentation**. Customer integration is handled commercially through the Lineage Link platform and through account teams, not through a self-service developer program. As of 2026-07-19 no OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL schema, SDK, CLI, MCP server, webhook catalog, sandbox, changelog or status page was found on any Lineage-operated host. This file exists so agents do not go looking for a developer surface that is not there.

## Products

- [Lineage Link](https://www.onelineage.com/lineage-link): Customer supply chain platform giving real-time visibility into inventory, orders, appointments and shipment status across the Lineage facility network; supports pickup/delivery scheduling, reporting and dashboards, inventory holds, and integration with customer WMS. Available across 200+ locations in eight countries.
- [Innovation & Technology](https://www.onelineage.com/about-us/innovation-technology): Overview of Lineage's proprietary technology, including Sybil (patented warehouse and inventory-placement optimization algorithm) and Lineage Eye (AI computer-vision automated receiving).
- [Services](https://www.onelineage.com/services): Cold storage warehousing, automated warehousing, built-to-suit warehousing, port-centric warehousing, blast freezing, food manufacturing and processing, direct-to-consumer fulfillment.
- [Global Integrated Solutions](https://www.onelineage.com/services/global-integrated-solutions): Freight forwarding, customs brokerage, drayage, managed transportation, temperature-controlled rail, LTL and multi-vendor consolidation.

## Identity surface

Lineage operates two verified OpenID Connect issuers behind its customer portals. Both are end-user portal SSO, not programmatic API authentication.

- [Lineage Link CIAM discovery](https://lineage-ciam.us.auth0.com/.well-known/openid-configuration): Auth0 tenant behind the custom domain ciam.lineageapps.com; supports authorization_code, client_credentials, refresh_token and device_code grants with PKCE (S256).
- [Lineage Link Help discovery](https://lineagelinkhelp.onelineage.com/.well-known/openid-configuration): Salesforce Experience Cloud issuer for the support community.
